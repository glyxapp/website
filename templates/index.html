Templates

Start from a finished dashboard.

Each template is a complete glyx.yml: pages, columns, widgets and theme. Open it in Glyx, look at what changes, and keep it or roll back from Settings.

  1. Open in GlyxThe app shows every page and widget it will add or remove.
  2. ApplyYour current glyx.yml is backed up first.
  3. Or downloadDrop the .yml file on the Glyx window, or copy parts into your own file.
