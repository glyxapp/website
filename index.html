Set up your way

Drag and resize widgets in the app, or write glyx.yml by hand. Either way it is one plain file, and a typo never wipes your setup.

Sandboxed plugins

Every widget runs in its own iframe and can only reach the hosts it declares. The Rust host checks every call.

Local-first

No Glyx server, no telemetry, no sign-in. Widgets only talk to the services they show data from, and the privacy page lists every request.

Configuration

Edit a file.
Watch it update.

Pages, columns and widgets, all in plain YAML. Back it up, diff it, keep it in your dotfiles or share it with a friend.

  • Hot reload in under a second, no restart
  • Safe errors: keeps the last good config and points to the line
  • Themes in dark, light or system, with your own accent
  • No file needed: the layout editor and Settings write it for you
%APPDATA%\Glyx\glyx.ymlReloaded
theme:
  preset: dark
  accent: "#7aa2f7"

window:
  mode: widget       # normal | top | widget | tray

pages:
  - name: Home
    columns:
      - size: small
        widgets:
          - plugin: glyx.clock
          - plugin: glyx.weather
            config: { location: Hanoi }
      - size: full
        widgets:
          - plugin: glyx.rss
            refresh: 15m
            config:
              feeds:
                - https://hnrss.org/frontpage

host.onRefresh(async () => {
  const { repo } = await host.config();
  const data = await host.fetchJson(
    `https://api.github.com/repos/${repo}`
  );
  stars.textContent = `★ ${data.stargazers_count}`;
});

Plugins

Every widget is a plugin.
Write yours in minutes.

A plugin is a folder with a manifest and a web page. The SDK gives you fetch with caching, storage, system stats and theme colors.

  • Declared permissions: a plugin can only call the hosts it lists
  • No direct network: raw fetch and WebSocket are blocked
  • Typed SDK with TypeScript definitions

Window modes

Out of the way until you need it.

Press Ctrl Alt G anywhere to show or hide your dashboard.

Windownormal

A regular app window on your taskbar.

A window that stays above your other apps.

Desktop widgetwidget

Borderless and translucent, pinned below your windows like a live wallpaper.

Tray popuptray

Lives in the tray. One click pops it up, and it hides when you click away.

Pills over your apps

Keep a number in sight while you work: the temperature, your Claude quota or a server's ping, floating small over any app.

Command palette

Ctrl K in Glyx, or Ctrl Shift G from any app, to search and act: add a note, log a spend, open a widget.

Mini apps

Notes, Vocabulary and the lunar calendar open full size, in a tab or in a window of their own.

An assistant

A small helper in the corner that reminds you to take a break and, with your own key, answers about your dashboard.
